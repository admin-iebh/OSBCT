How to judge

Each item shows a canon paragraph as the reader saw it, with the word they clicked marked. Below it is one row the panel put in a box at the top, and the claim the panel made in putting it there. Judge whether that claim earns the row its place.

A — the claim holds and the row helps herethe promotion is right: this really is the edition explaining what is in front of the reader
B — the claim holds but the row is beside the point heretechnically true — the phrase does stand here, or the edition does gloss this form — but it explains a different phrase, a different sense, or says almost nothing
C — the claim does not holdthe promotion is wrong: the phrase is not really here (a spurious stem match), or the row is not the commentary on this paragraph at all

Notes are worth more than the letter — especially where you hesitate. Judgements save in this page as you go; press Export verdicts at the end.
