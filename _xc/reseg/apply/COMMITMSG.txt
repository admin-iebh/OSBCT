## The gate that failed first, and why it is not loosened

`verify_render_vs_pdf` read **7 printed lines missing from the render** on the
first pass.  They are exactly the four `verse/` entries that carry no `groups`
key — the ones `reader2.html:1474` has never drawn (phase 2 §3; 75 such entries
in 30 volumes, a shipped defect independent of this work).  The prepared map
dropped them "as the shipped reader drops them", but the shipped MAP did not
drop them, and the harness measures the map.

They are carried through instead, keyed by the printed position `b2/locate`
found for them (before ords 15, 29, 99, 511), in their shipped shape and with
**no `groups` key**, so the browser still ignores them exactly as today.  The
defect is preserved unchanged for the separate fix phase 2 §7.6 names; no data
is lost and nothing new is drawn.  RESTORE=1 was NOT used: it would have put
189 letters on the page that the reader has never shown, which is a content
decision this commit is not the place for.

## Apparatus: two routes, and a third to adjudicate them

`rebuild_apparatus.py` was re-run against the re-segmented corpus with the
existing appk emptied first, so its MERGE branch could not re-attach notes at
old-ordinal keys.  Result: **520 printed notes anchored, 1 unanchored**
(p178 note 7 `Icchitabbato (Ka)`, a duplicate text whose other copy anchored).

Cross-checked against `_xc/reseg/apparatus/` (the redistribution route):

    same 520 notes both ways — 0 only-in-A, 0 only-in-B, 516 distinct (n,text)
    same ordinal:  392 of 516  (76.0%)
    disputed:      124

The 124 were put to a THIRD route that consults neither — the page the
footnote cell is printed on, the marker's own position in that page's body
letters, and the paragraph whose printed extent contains it
(`_xc/reseg/apply/adjudicate.py`):

    rebuild_apparatus right  70      redistribution right  41
    neither  3               undecided  10

So the shipped route is right on 462 of 516 (89.5%) by printed position,
against 433 (84.0%) for the redistribution.  It is the better route and it is
the one shipped — but 41 notes are placed where the third route disagrees, and
that is recorded rather than patched by hand.

## Baselines: NONE MOVED

`check_links.py`, `check_ordinal.py` and `check_concordance.py` were expected
to move and did not — every measure is identical to the digit:

    n-match 55.196%  name-match 76.195%  reachable 28145      (check_links)
    ord_match 86.987%  byname 99.892%  condemned 320          (check_ordinal)
    targets 67323  outside 3163  violation 4.698%             (check_concordance)

The reason is that the remap is 1:1 and monotone onto the FIRST of each run,
and the first of a run is the paragraph that carries the printed number, so
every number, name and volume the three gates read is the same paragraph's.
`--record` was therefore not run, and `pipeline/*_baseline.json` is untouched.

## NOT VERIFIED

* **`pipeline/check_layout.js` was NOT run.**  It needs Playwright, which is not
  installed on the device and cannot be, as there is no network there.  Nothing
  in this commit is browser-verified.  The render equivalence that stands behind
  it is a RE-IMPLEMENTATION of `reader2.html`'s order and its `vmap.groups`
  gate, not the reader itself.
* `reader2.html:1474` gates the verse branch on `kind==='canon'` as well as on
  `vmap.groups`.  A commentary volume drawn in the A band therefore never takes
  that branch at all, so the verse map's effect in the paired view is untested
  here in either direction.
* `apparatus/20KhuA01.app.json` is the legacy id-keyed input, read only by
  `rekey_apparatus.py` and served to nobody.  It is left as it was: 57 of its
  58 keys still resolve against the new ids, 58 with the base-indexing fix the
  blocker-1 note describes.  Do not run `rekey_apparatus.py` on this volume.
