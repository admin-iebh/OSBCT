Three more Khuddaka commentaries are re-segmented: 21KhuA02, 23KhuA04 and
24KhuA05 go from 351 paragraphs to 2,934, and five defects in the method had to
be fixed before they could

The first transfer of the 20KhuA01 method (`75ee5904`) to other volumes, run as
an experiment with three subjects rather than a rollout.  The method transfers,
but NOT unchanged: five defects surfaced, three of which would have shipped
silently corrupted volumes, and each is fixed in `_xc/reseg2/gen.py` as a named,
asserted patch over the phase-1/2 scripts rather than a rewrite of them.

## The five defects the transfer exposed

1. `sections_by_print` started its cursor at letter 0, inside the FRONT MATTER.
   23KhuA04 prints a full contents listing before its body, and the cursor
   locked onto it: 68 of 89 headings anchored to the contents page — and
   `check_before` returned **ok on every one of them**, because the contents
   page really is printed before paragraph 0.  A check that passes on wrong
   input is not a check.  The cursor now starts at the body.
2. The same function retried the search FROM ZERO when a heading was not found.
   That is how one bad match poisoned the cursor for everything after it.  An
   unlocatable heading is now REFUSED and left at first-of-run (8 vagga headings
   in 23KhuA04, 4 nipāta headings in 24KhuA05, 7 in 21KhuA02 — none of them is
   printed in the body at all).
3. A straddle was decided from a block's two ENDPOINTS.  A block that begins in
   paragraph P, swallows the whole of P+1 and ends in a gap has no straddling
   endpoint, so P+1 was neither merged nor hidden and went on rendering itself:
   24KhuA05 drew `Madhuram imaṁ paguṇaṁ suvibhattaṁ` TWICE.  A straddle is now
   every paragraph whose printed extent INTERSECTS the block.
4. A `groups` block moved into `before`/`after` was emitted as a bare LIST — a
   shape no consumer accepts.  `rebuild_apparatus.py:189` crashed on it, and
   reader2's `proseOne` would not have drawn it.  **The letter-equivalence check
   did not see this**: `btxt` fell through to `json.dumps`, whose punctuation the
   letters filter strips, so the letters matched while the shape was wrong.  118
   blocks in 21KhuA02, that volume alone in the corpus.  A shape check is now run
   over every emitted block.
5. `uddana/` last-of-run does not generalise.  On 21KhuA02 it locates only 27 of
   113 blocks after their anchor and misses 85, because the Dhammapada
   commentary closes every vatthu with a colophon that a shipped lump swallows
   text past.  `uddana/` is now anchored to the paragraph it follows ON THE
   PAGE; unlocatable blocks are refused and left at last-of-run.

A sixth, smaller one: a no-`groups` verse entry must be carried through in the
slot its PRINTED POSITION gives it, not the slot the shipped entry used.  Under
the lumps `before` and `after` were the same place; they are not any more.
