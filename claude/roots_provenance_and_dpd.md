# Roots, provenance, and DPD (RECONSTRUCTION — see README.md)

§9 of the project instructions governs this and quotes this file's
conclusion: RENAMING A MODERN DICTIONARY'S ANALYSIS INTO THIS PROJECT'S
NOTATION DOES NOT MAKE IT THIS PROJECT'S ANALYSIS.

Pāḷi roots are cited in the Pāḷi grammatical tradition with their final
vowel / anubandha — **√gamu**, not √gam.  DPD (and most modern lexica) cite
the Sanskritised form.  Converting √gam to √gamu mechanically and
presenting the result as this project's derivation would launder DPD's
analysis through our notation: the provenance principle (working principle
4) forbids it.

What IS permitted: DPD and the other modern dictionaries as reference and
for learning Pāḷi, shown under their own names with their own source lines
(the word-lookup panel), never merged into the Gloss tab, which is the
edition speaking in its own voice.  Grammatical authority remains
Kaccāyana, Rūpasiddhi and the Saddanīti's Dhātumālā; lexical authority the
Tipiṭaka-Pāḷi-Myanmā-Abhidhāna.
